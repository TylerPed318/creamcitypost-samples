What is mudjacking?

Don't replace it. Raise it.

Mudjacking raises sunken concrete back to its original level, restoring uneven slabs close to their original grade and alignment with the concrete around them. Here's how it works.

Drill

A number of 1.5-inch holes are drilled in the sunken concrete.

Pump

Slurry is pumped through the holes. When the void underneath is filled, the concrete rises.

Patch & use

The holes are patched with shrink-proof grout and the surface is ready to use.

“The cost of mudjacking averages a fraction of the cost of replacement. Mudjacking is faster, cleaner, and more cost effective.”
