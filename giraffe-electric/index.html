Our story

A family shop that grew up wiring homes.

Frank Jrolf started Giraffe Electric doing residential wiring, running the business out of the family's New Berlin home. His sons, John and Jeff, began their electrical training under his guidance in childhood.

After Frank's passing, John (President) and Jeff (Vice-President) re-incorporated the company as Giraffe Electric II. Today it's a team of journeymen, master electricians, apprentices and a CAD technician, and every apprentice goes through apprenticeship schooling with ABC Wisconsin.
