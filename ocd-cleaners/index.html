Our story

It all began with a neighbor.

“Back in 2000, I saw my neighbor, Mary, standing in her driveway completely frustrated.” Her husband was bringing someone home for dinner, the house was a mess and the kids were underfoot, so Ginger jumped in and helped. “The next morning, Mary came over and asked if I could help her every week and that’s how OCD Cleaners started.”

Since then the company has built long-standing relationships with realtors, construction companies, manufacturing businesses, and office building managers, and it still runs as a family owned and operated business.

“I chose the name OCD Cleaners because I know firsthand the pain and struggle of living with Obsessive Compulsive Disorder. Rather than letting my diagnosis overwhelm, discourage or embarrass me, I’ve decided to turn my difficulties into skills that help others.”
Ginger, on the About page
