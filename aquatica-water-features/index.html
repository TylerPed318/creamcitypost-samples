Where do you start?

Build it, keep it running, or stock it.

Aquatica designs and builds the water feature, services it afterward, and sells the koi, plants and parts from the shop in Wales. Pick the one you need.

Design and build

Ponds, koi and fish ponds, pondless waterfalls, streams, bubbling rocks and water gardens, with pond lighting. Projects start with an on-site design consultation.
