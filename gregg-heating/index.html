About Gregg

A New Berlin shop since 1974.

Gregg Heating & Air Conditioning was established in 1974 by Greg Hausinger. Kyle Geisberger, the current owner, began working at Gregg Heating in 2001 and took over in 2017 when Greg retired. Most new business comes from satisfied clients and referrals — and the shop still stands behind every job from 2165 S 170th St in New Berlin.
